⚪ Fluid Go ⚫

Black's Turn

How to Play

  • Goal: Surround the opponent's stones with your continuous fluid influence to capture them.
  • Desktop: Left Click to place. Hold Shift to place continuously instead of snapping to grid.
  • Touch: Drag to preview placement, release to place. Use "Snap: ON/OFF" button to toggle free placement.
  • Connectivity: Stones close to each other merge into a single connected group.
  • Capture Rule: A group survives as long as its boundary is not completely submerged by the opponent's fluid force field.
